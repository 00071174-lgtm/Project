Who we are

A gym built around coaching, not just equipment

Amcol Fitness opened its doors to give Southern Trinidad a health club that treats members like athletes, not subscriptions. Our coaches design every class around proper technique first, results second — because the two go hand in hand. Whether your goal is weight loss, strength, mobility, or simply a consistent routine, our team meets you where you are and builds a path forward.

Before you train

House rules for members & guests

  1. Re-rack all weights and wipe down equipment after use.
  2. Closed-toe athletic shoes are required on the gym floor at all times.
  3. Guests must sign in at reception and are limited to two visits before membership is required.
  4. Please respect posted class capacities — book your spot in advance where possible.
  5. Amcol Fitness is a judgement-free space: all fitness levels and goals are welcome.
