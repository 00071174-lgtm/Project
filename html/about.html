Our Story

About Amcol Fitness

Founded by a team of trainers who wanted Southern Trinidad to have a serious strength and conditioning facility, Amcol Fitness has grown into a full health club without losing its coaching-first roots.

Our approach

Health, strength and community

We believe fitness should be sustainable, not punishing. Every class, program and membership tier at Amcol Fitness is built around long-term consistency — proper movement, realistic goals, and a community that keeps you accountable. Our facility spans a full strength floor, dedicated studio space for group classes, and a recovery area for stretching and mobility work.
